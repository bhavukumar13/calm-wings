## From Airport Entrance to Aircraft Cabin

This document outlines the complete end-to-end journey of a student traveling through an airport, starting from the terminal entrance and ending when they are seated inside the aircraft.

---

# User Journey Overview

| Stage | Student Actions | Thoughts | Pain Points | Opportunities |
|--------|-----------------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **1. Arrival at Airport Entrance** | Arrives by taxi, bus, metro, or with family. Collects luggage and approaches the entrance. | "Am I at the correct terminal?" | Confusing terminal layouts, traffic, heavy baggage. | Better signage, digital navigation, luggage assistance. |
| **2. Terminal Entry Security** | Shows boarding pass/ticket and ID to enter the terminal. Passes through initial security screening. | "Hope I have all my documents." | Long queues, document confusion. | QR-based entry, queue management, staff assistance. |
| **3. Enter Terminal** | Enters the departure hall and looks around. | "Where do I go now?" | Large, unfamiliar environment. | Interactive maps, multilingual signage. |
| **4. Flight Information Display (FIDS)** | Checks departure screens for airline, check-in counter, and gate details. | "Which counter should I go to?" | Difficulty locating information. | Mobile notifications, larger displays, searchable kiosks. |
| **5. Walk to Check-in Counter** | Navigates to the assigned airline counter with luggage. | "Hope I'm not late." | Long walking distances. | Better wayfinding, moving walkways. |
| **6. Check-in Queue** | Waits in line for airline check-in. | "This line is taking forever." | Long wait times. | Queue prediction, self-service kiosks. |
| **7. Check-in Process** | Presents ID/passport, checks baggage, receives boarding pass if not already checked in online. | "Is my baggage within the weight limit?" | Excess baggage fees, document issues. | Smart baggage scales, digital boarding passes. |
| **8. Baggage Drop** | Hands over checked luggage. | "Will my luggage reach safely?" | Fear of lost baggage. | Real-time baggage tracking. |
| **9. Walk to Security Check** | Follows signs to the security screening area. | "Need to prepare my electronics and liquids." | Forgetting prohibited items. | Reminder displays before security. |
| **10. Security Queue** | Waits in the security line. | "This is stressful." | Long queues. | Live wait-time estimates, reservation systems. |
| **11. Security Screening** | Places belongings in trays, walks through scanner, retrieves items afterward. | "Hope nothing gets flagged." | Delays, repacking belongings. | Larger repacking areas, clearer instructions. |
| **12. Collect Belongings** | Reorganizes luggage and confirms all belongings are collected. | "Did I leave anything behind?" | Forgotten personal items. | Reminder systems near exits. |
| **13. Immigration (International Flights)** | Presents passport and visa to immigration officer. | "Everything should be okay." | Long waiting times, documentation issues. | Automated e-gates where available. |
| **14. Airside Terminal Experience** | Visits restaurants, shops, lounges, charging stations, or restrooms. | "I have some time before boarding." | Expensive food, limited seating. | Student discounts, more charging stations, quiet study areas. |
| **15. Monitor Boarding Information** | Regularly checks departure boards or app for gate updates. | "Has my gate changed?" | Last-minute gate changes. | Push notifications through airport apps. |
| **16. Walk to Boarding Gate** | Travels to the assigned gate. | "It's farther than I expected." | Long walking distances. | Indoor navigation assistance. |
| **17. Wait at Boarding Gate** | Waits for boarding while studying, using phone, or calling family. | "When will boarding start?" | Crowded waiting areas. | Comfortable seating, charging ports, study spaces. |
| **18. Boarding Announcement** | Listens for boarding group announcements and prepares documents. | "Don't miss my boarding group." | Confusing announcements. | Visual boarding indicators, app notifications. |
| **19. Boarding Queue** | Joins boarding line according to assigned group. | "Hope there's room for my cabin bag." | Crowding and slow movement. | More efficient boarding strategies. |
| **20. Boarding Pass Scan** | Boarding pass is scanned before entering the aircraft. | "Almost there." | Scanner errors. | NFC/mobile wallet compatibility. |
| **21. Jet Bridge or Shuttle Bus** | Walks through the jet bridge or rides a shuttle bus to the aircraft. | "Finally!" | Crowding, weather exposure when using buses. | Better passenger flow management. |
| **22. Aircraft Entry** | Flight attendants greet passengers at the aircraft door. | "Where's my seat?" | Congestion near the entrance. | Cabin guidance by crew. |
| **23. Find Seat & Store Cabin Bag** | Locates assigned seat and stores luggage in overhead bin. | "Hope there's enough overhead space." | Full overhead compartments. | Improved cabin baggage management. |
| **24. Seated in Aircraft Cabin** | Sits down, fastens seatbelt, stores personal items, prepares for departure. | "I'm ready for the journey." | Limited legroom, settling in. | Better seat organization, digital seat information. |
